Where Retirology is different

  • No sales funnel. There's no advisory business behind Retirology and nothing to upsell. You pay once and own the app.
  • No account, no linked logins. Your financial data stays in a file on your own computer.
  • Depth on the tax side. The decisions that move an early retirement — Roth conversions, ACA subsidies, penalty-free early access, state taxes, IRMAA — are modeled together every year of the plan.

When Empower is the better choice

  • You mainly want automatic net-worth and investment-fee tracking, for free.
  • You're interested in having a firm manage your investments.
