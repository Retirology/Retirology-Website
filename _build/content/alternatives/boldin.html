Where Retirology is different

  • No subscription, ever. Pay once from $19 and get every future version, including the yearly tax refresh.
  • No account and no aggregator. Your balances, income and plans live in one file on your computer. There's no login to breach and no financial credentials to hand over.
  • Early-retirement depth. Roth ladders that respect the 2026 ACA subsidy cliff, 72(t) SEPP sizing, the Rule of 55, IRMAA, survivor years and every state's age-based retirement breaks — modeled together, year by year.
  • One-click optimization. Optimize my plan tests your ladder, ACA target and SEPP together and explains each recommendation in after-tax dollars.

When Boldin is the better choice

  • You want balances to update automatically from your bank and brokerage accounts.
  • You want access from any device, or a document vault for your financial paperwork.
  • You'd like the option of a session with a human advisor.
