How they fit together

A budgeting app tells you what you spend. A retirement planner tells you what that spending means: how big your target is, when you'll reach it, and how to draw the money down without overpaying tax. Retirology's Budget Analysis turns a category-by-category budget into your FI target and date, so the numbers from your budget app have somewhere to go.

When Monarch is the better choice

  • You want every transaction synced and categorized, shared with a partner.
  • Your main goal right now is controlling day-to-day spending.
