Where Retirology is different

  • Still a one-time purchase. $19+ once, with every future version free.
  • No spreadsheet required. A native app with a setup wizard, charts, a cash-flow diagram and explanations in plain English.
  • Optimize my plan. Instead of hand-tuning settings, let the app test combinations of Roth ladder, ACA target and SEPP and show the after-tax result of each change.
  • Private by design. Like Gold, your data stays on your computer — without needing Excel.

When Pralana is the better choice

  • You want to control every assumption and are happy to learn a large model.
  • You live in Excel and want to inspect or extend the formulas.
