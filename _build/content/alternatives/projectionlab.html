Where Retirology is different

  • You pay once. $19 is the floor, $65 is suggested, and every future version — including each year's tax-table refresh — is free. There's nothing to renew.
  • Your plan never leaves your machine. No sign-up, no login, no cloud copy of your net worth. The app makes one network request per launch: a check for updates.
  • Built around US early-retirement tax moves. Roth ladders, the 2026 ACA subsidy cliff, 72(t) SEPP and the Rule of 55, IRMAA, and every state's 2026 rules for Social Security and retirement income — applied year by year, by age.
  • Optimize my plan. One click tries your Roth ladder strategy, ACA income target and SEPP choice together and explains each change it recommends in after-tax dollars.

When ProjectionLab is the better choice

  • You want to use your plan from any browser, or on your phone.
  • You're planning across countries.
  • You want to share your plan with a financial advisor or coach who uses ProjectionLab.
  • You want to model your whole financial life in visual detail, not mainly the US retirement-tax side.

Plenty of people use both: a broad life plan in one, and the tax-heavy retirement drawdown in the other.
