Where Retirology is different

  • Pay once. No annual renewal; every future version is free.
  • Local and private. No account; your plan stays in a file on your computer.
  • Built for retiring early. Getting at Traditional money before 59½ (Roth ladders, SEPP, Rule of 55) while managing the 2026 ACA subsidy cliff is the core of the app, not an add-on.
  • Starts from your real budget. Spending comes from a category-by-category budget, with lifestyle changes over time, and drives a plan-based FI target and date.

When MaxiFi is the better choice

  • You want an economics-based answer to "how much can I safely spend every year for life?"
  • Social Security claiming strategy is your main question and you want the most thorough optimizer for it.
  • You want life-insurance planning built in.
